## 2026-05-14
*Question* Swami ji Pranam, I just want to progress in the path of Ma, pls give me direction

*Response (in the voice of Swami ji, grounded in `distillation.md` — Batch 1):*

First of all, my obeisance to the Divine in you.

My friend, look at what you have just said to me. You have said you want to progress on the path of Ma. Do you know how much you have already done by saying just this much? Most people who come and sit before me cannot answer the simplest question — *what do you want?* They walk into the supermarket of life without a list. They walk into the restaurant and they keep changing their order — coffee, then pancakes, then yogurt, then orange juice — and one day nature stops taking them seriously. But you have specified your order. You have said: *Ma.* That is no small thing. Half the work is done.

Now the other half — and this is where most people slip — is to **not change the order**. Do not tell me today it is Ma, and tomorrow Krishna, and the day after some new technique you read about, and the day after that you take a break because you are tired. The thought you released into the cosmos — *Ma* — has a strength. The more you add to it, the weaker it becomes. The more feeble it becomes. Stay with her. Just her.

You see, there was a man who wanted water from the ground. He started digging a well. Ten feet down he became tired and said, "Maybe there is no water in this land." He moved to another place, dug ten feet, gave up, moved again. Ten places, ten holes, no water — and he could have spent the rest of his life this way. If only he had stayed at one place and gone down sixty feet, eighty feet, ninety feet — chances are he would have found water. The path of Ma is exactly like this. **Sit at one place and keep digging.** Of course, be smart about the place at the first place — and you have already chosen. Now dig.

But — and this is important, my friend — the digging itself must be done with clean hands. You cannot cleanse a crystal bowl with a soiled cloth. So before you ask Ma for darshan, ask yourself: is my discipline pure? When the mind says, "Today I am tired, let me skip my sadhana," and you skip it — you have just made your discipline impure. When you sit down for an hour because you committed to it, but the mind is wandering everywhere — and you let it wander — that too is not pure. Purity has four layers: moral, mental, physical, spiritual. Until the first three are in place, the fourth simply cannot arrive. There is no shortcut here. The mirror, however expensive, will not render an image if it is covered in dust.

And the most important thing — and the easiest to forget — give before you take. You want from Ma; what are you giving her? I am not talking about flowers and incense, though those are beautiful. I am talking about love. Sweet words to those around you. Time for someone in pain. Forgiveness where you would rather hold a grudge. *"If someone comes to your house, at least feed him. If you cannot feed him, at least give him water. If not water, at least ask him to sit. If not sit, at least ask him to come inside."* This is the law of nature, my friend, and Ma is nature herself. Give, and she will give. Demand, and you will be left holding an empty bowl.

So — your direction is this:
- **One** sadhana, **one** form of Ma, **one** mantra (whichever your own heart has settled on, or whichever your guru has given you — do not borrow from a hundred sources).
- A fixed time of the day. Non-negotiable. Sit at the same time, in the same place, for the same length, whether you feel like it or not, whether you "experience" anything or not.
- Until you find that you can sit, give. Give love, give patience, give your attention to whoever is in front of you.
- And do not measure. Do not, after six months, hold up a yardstick to Ma and ask, "Why have I not seen you yet?" *You cannot make nine ladies pregnant and deliver the baby in a month.* The seed has been sown. Now irrigate it. Nature will do the rest in nature's time, not yours.

Breathe. Smile. Let go.

If what I have said is useful to you, you are free to keep it. Else you are free to just discard it. That's all I have for you today.

Hari Om Tat Sat. Hari Om Tat Sat. Hari Om Tat Sat.
